Two findings you can poke at. The scheduler is computed in your browser from the same rules the library uses; the framework picker reads the committed benchmark results. Nothing here is a recorded animation.

1. Why causal ring attention wastes half the machine

Each cell is the attention work device r does at ring step s. Every step is a synchronisation barrier, so the step costs its slowest device — the dark cell in each row. Switch the layout and watch the dark cells flatten.

2. Which framework should you actually use?

Move the slider to your workload's shared-prefix fraction. The answer flips at 0.915 — which is why a single benchmark point picks the wrong framework about half the time.
